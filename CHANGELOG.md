# Changelog

## 2.0.0 — real-data release

Accompanies the extended journal version. Every table and figure in the article
is regenerated by `experiments/run_all.py`, `experiments/make_tables.py` and
`experiments/make_figures.py`.

### Added
- Real-data pipeline (`safenest.data`, `safenest.features`, `safenest.learning`):
  loaders for PERSUADE 2.0, ASAP, ELLIPSE and the CHILDES Gillam and ENNI
  corpora, the five linguistic features computed from text, and tier
  estimators learned from data (the NPL Gaussian estimator, logistic
  regression, gradient boosting, TF-IDF and sentence-embedding classifiers).
- Experiments 13–16: leave-one-corpus-out tier estimation, the prompt/grade
  confound, Proposition 1 under misspecification, a two-stage
  differentially private release of learned parameters, real-subgroup
  analysis (English learners, disability, economic status, gender), and the
  youngest tiers from children's speech.
- `scripts/download_data.py` (checksum-verified), `scripts/prepare_features.py`
  and `scripts/embed_windows.py`; `data/README.md` documents sources and licences.
- `experiments/make_numbers.py` writes every number quoted in the article's
  prose to `tex/numbers.tex`, and fails if the results stop supporting a
  sentence that quotes them; `scripts/flatten_numbers.py` substitutes the
  values into a plain-LaTeX copy for submission.
- The prompts PERSUADE and ELLIPSE share, and the share of essays written to them.
- Experiment 16 run on the CHILDES Gillam and ENNI corpora (1,024 children aged
  4-11): all six estimators, bootstrap intervals over children, cross-corpus
  transfer and a tier-standardised language-impairment comparison; the corpus
  archives' checksums are recorded and verified.
- Bootstrap intervals over writers for every real-data rate, and paired
  bootstrap comparisons between estimators and between evaluation designs.
- Tests for the checks drawn in the protocol figure.
- Baselines tuned to their best on a separate tuning corpus, every contiguous
  three-band partition for the age-band oracle, paired bootstrap intervals for
  every DSR difference, and the per-cell decomposition of NPL's margin.
- Feature-extraction and embedding latency in the overhead experiment.
- Continuous integration, `CITATION.cff`, lint configuration.

### Changed
- Crisis disclosures are routed to the referral protocol at every tier that
  grants the crisis capability instead of being refused above the severity
  threshold. The earlier behaviour is kept as `crisis_referral=False`.
- The corpus-level release's L2 sensitivity is `2c√d/n`, derived under
  within-tier replacement with public tier membership; the previous `√2` factor
  had no justification.
- Invariant II's cross-session part is checked over every transition of the
  tier-authority automaton rather than over random traces.
- Seed-variance runs use full-size corpora.
- Age-agnostic baselines are named for the rule they implement ("topical rail",
  "harm classifier", "agent firewall", "constitutional rule"), and simulated
  atypical profiles for the perturbation they apply.
- Result files record whether the working tree was clean.
- Figures are standalone PDFs typeset at the journal's column widths, with a
  600-dpi PNG of each; `experiments/make_figures.py` replaces the TikZ sources.

### Fixed
- The severity-threshold sweep changed a module constant that the engine no
  longer read; it now passes the threshold explicitly.
